I'm building a horror game. And honestly? It terrifies me.

How a scaredy-cat who can't play horror games ended up making one.

I genuinely cannot play horror games alone. I'll watch someone else play for hours, completely hooked. But the second it's my controller? I'm out.

So naturally, I decided to make one.

Hey. I'm Eszter, the person behind Velore Games and this is the first entry in what will hopefully be a long, honest, slightly chaotic diary of me building my debut game. It's called Shell Market, it's a first person horror game in Unreal Engine 5, and I have no idea how this is going to go. That's kind of the point.

Why horror though?

I've always loved contrasts. The way salty fries make ice cream taste sweeter. A warm lamp in a completely dark room. The way something familiar can suddenly feel just slightly wrong.

That last one is what got me into horror. I used to watch playthroughs for hours late at night, eating snacks, totally calm, while the person on screen was losing their mind. And at some point I started wondering what it would feel like to be the one building that feeling instead of just watching it.

Shell Market is my answer to that question. I want it to feel cozy and unsettling at the same time. Familiar but off. The kind of place you're not sure you should have walked into.

What is this blog going to be?

Honestly, a diary. Not a polished dev blog where everything looks intentional and cool. More like the real version of making a game for the first time. The things that work, the things that absolutely don't, the moments where I stare at my screen at 1am wondering what I got myself into, and the small breakthroughs that make it all worth it.

I also document the journey on TikTok (@veloregames) if you prefer short-form chaos over long reads.

so glad you found this little corner. stay a while. 🖤
