About the game

One night, a little star falls from the sky and breaks into pieces. In a cozy, peaceful town, you take on the role of Yura, who sets out to collect the lost star fragments and help it gather enough strength to find its way back home.

Wander through charming streets, soak in the soothing atmosphere, and watch as the star slowly regains its light. It's a simple yet heartfelt journey about care, warmth, and the quiet beauty of small moments, one you can take entirely at your own pace.

  • ✦ A relaxing and cozy experience
  • ✦ Explore a peaceful town at your own pace
  • ✦ Bring the fallen star back to life

The story behind it

With Yura I wanted to try something completely different. Instead of adding more and more, I went the other way. I kept only what the game really needs and let everything else fall away. The whole world is painted to feel like watercolor, soft and a little imperfect, the kind of look that says a lot with very few details.

That restraint became the point. I wasn't chasing realism or a long list of features, I was chasing a feeling. Something calm, gentle, and quietly a bit strange. Leaning fully into the mood, and letting the washed out colors and loose shapes carry it, taught me how far a clear art direction can go on its own.

Yura is a small, personal piece, and that is exactly what I wanted it to be.
