About the game

Shadows embody our deepest and darkest emotions. If you try to get rid of them, they'll do anything to catch up with you. Remember, your shadow is only ever a few steps away, and it'll catch up faster than you think.

So… what's your shadow?

The team & the jam

What's your Shadow was made during the HTK GameJam, Ice Edition 2022, as a school project.

Our team consisted of six creative HTK students who worked together to bring the game to life. My part was the idea finding, along with the pixel art and animation that gave the shadows their movement.
