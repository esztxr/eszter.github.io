About the game

You've just found yourself back in your lovely swamp, home sweet home. But wait… what is this? These robots look just like you, and they just keep coming! So grab what you've got and protect your little hut. Frog Off is a tower defence game about holding your ground.

Upgrade your towers with coins so you don't get overwhelmed by enemies, while a soundtrack that follows your level progress keeps you constantly on edge. Protect your home as long as possible and survive while protecting. The longer you last, the higher you'll place on the highscore list.

The 4 days

Frog Off was made during a four-day game jam hosted by the HTK Academy, the 2024 edition, built around the theme "Frogpunk."

I was the art lead on the project: I handled the general art and 3D, the level design, and a big part of the idea finding, shaping how the whole swamp came together.
