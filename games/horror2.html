What's coming

Shell Market is my debut game, made in Unreal Engine. You work in a little shop, and at first it feels lovely. Calm, quiet, the kind of place you'd happily spend your evenings. But that peace is deceiving, and the longer you stay, the more you start to notice that something isn't quite right.

It's turning into a mix of cozy and horror, because putting two things that don't belong together has always fascinated me. Something warm and unsettling at the same time. Familiar, but a little off.

If you want to know more, come read the blog, follow along on TikTok, or just say hi on Discord. ❤
