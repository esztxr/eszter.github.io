About the game

Robo Jobo Inc. is a management game with attitude. Every day a fresh batch of robots clocks in, and it's your job to match each one with the work it was built for. In a world that runs on cold retrofuturistic efficiency, there's no room for mistakes.

Your daily duty is to provide robots with suitable jobs, giving them meaning in life by choosing the right robo job with your trusted skills. You can decide between 5 job fields with 3 subcategories each. Should you send the chef back to its kitchen, or should he start a new career as a V-Tuber? It's up to you, and only you. The 4 days

Robo Jobo Inc. was created in four days for the HTK Game Jam 2024, Fire Edition, built around the theme "Retrofuturism."

I made the game together with five other people. My focus was on writing the in-game text and creating the 3D assets, giving the whole robotic world its story and its look.

For those four days I had a wonderful team, where everyone got to show and live out their strengths, and everyone helped everyone else. It was just such a good feeling. All in all, simply a lovely, creative, and really fun game jam.
