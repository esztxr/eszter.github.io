About Me

Hey there, I'm Eszter :)

Even before I could walk, I was holding a controller, playing games with my older brother. Since then, I've been fascinated by how games tell stories and pull us into completely different worlds.

My love for books also inspired me early on to write my own stories and create imaginative universes. Even back then, I dreamed of one day bringing those worlds to life through games.

Over the past three years, I've learned how to make that dream real: how to turn ideas into experiences, express emotion through gameplay, and design worlds that players can truly lose themselves in.

That's why I've decided to make games of my own, and to let players dive right into the worlds I create.

Along the way, I've not only found my path but also my passion. Every day, I try to grow a little more and get closer to turning those dreams into reality.
